120+ Years of Glory

Legendary Eras of Madridismo

From the dusty pitches of Madrid in 1902 to global football supremacy, discover the chapters that defined white majesty.

1902 - 1950

The Royal Origins & Don Santiago

Founded as Madrid Football Club on March 6, 1902. King Alfonso XIII granted the 'Real' title in 1920. Santiago Bernabéu transformed the club into an international powerhouse.

1955 - 1960

The Di Stéfano Golden 5-In-A-Row

Alfredo Di Stéfano, Ferenc Puskás, and Raymond Kopa dominated the newly formed European Champion Clubs' Cup, winning the first 5 consecutive European titles in history.

2000 - 2018

Los Galácticos & Historic 3-Peat

Florentino Pérez ushered in the Galáctico era (Zidane, Ronaldo, Figo, Beckham). Later led by Cristiano Ronaldo & Zidane, Real Madrid achieved an unprecedented Champions League 3-peat.
